TWO EQUAL HASHES BURN · ONE OF DOUBLE THE BITS IS WRITTEN · THE RED SEAM MARKS THE FUSION

Merging is free, gas only, and unlimited while you hold a pair. Both hashes must sit in the same wallet. The new hash is written with a red seam on its artwork and a fresh serial. Two 2048-bit hashes cannot merge: a complete SHA-256 is the roof.

05 · TOKEN

$HASH, behind the gate.

THE TOKEN · FIXED SUPPLY 2,048,000,000

Hashes mint the token

There is exactly one way to mint $HASH: hold a hash. Every hash carries a minting allowance of 1,000,000 $HASH per unit it represents. A 2-bit hash mints 1,000,000. A 32-bit hash mints 16,000,000. A complete 2048-bit hash mints 1,024,000,000, half the supply in a single key.

Minting never burns the hash, and an allowance can be spent only once per hash. Merge two hashes and the child inherits whatever its parents already minted, so no unit ever pays out twice. Token minted so far: 0 / 2,048,000,000

06 · RECORD

Contracts.

HASDATA (NFT)

One ERC-721 contract holds the whole game: minting, the boost, merging, and the unit cap of 2,048. Mint proceeds collect in the contract for the owner. A second contract issues the $HASH token, mintable by hash holders only (section 05). No presale.

Contract address: published here at launch, before mint opens.
